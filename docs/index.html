How it works

About a minute, start to finish

  1. Open LanCast

    Run lancast.exe on your PC, with your TV on and on the same network.

  2. Click Cast

    Pick your TV. The first time, accept the request on the TV.

  3. Press OK

    On the TV remote, for sound and full screen.

"Windows protected your PC"? Click More info, then Run anyway. LanCast isn't code-signed yet.

Windows Firewall asks? Click Allow, so the TV can reach the PC.

Requirements

Most PCs from recent years

PC

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • NVIDIA, AMD or Intel graphics with video encoding
  • Wired network recommended; Wi-Fi works

TV

  • Any smart TV with a web browser
  • LG webOS opens automatically (the only TVs tested so far)
  • Phones, tablets, computers: open the link

Questions

Technical details ↗

Is it free?

Yes. Open source, Apache 2.0. No ads, no accounts.

Does it use the internet?

No. Video goes straight from your PC to your TV on your home network.

How much delay?

On an LG TV, about a tenth to a quarter of a second, plus the TV's own processing. Use the TV's Game mode.

TV not in the list?

Check it's on and on the same network, and that the firewall was allowed. Or type the link LanCast shows into the TV's browser.

Picture stutters?

Choose Balanced, or Wi-Fi if either device is wireless.

No sound?

Press OK on the remote. ◀ ▶ shift the sound if it's out of sync.

Who can watch?

Anyone on your network with the link, unless you change Who can watch.

Mac or Linux?

Planned.
